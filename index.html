Vocabulary Cards

写下想记住的英文单词、短语或句子，也可以直接用中文问怎么说。Cue 用 AI 写好词条、配一张图、读给你听，再用间隔复习帮你记住。

常见问题

点数怎么算？

一个新词约 1 点（词条和图片），一个问题 0.3 到 0.5 点，别人添加过的词 0.2 点。点数不会过期。也可以在设置里改用自己的 OpenAI 密钥，费用由 OpenAI 直接向你收取。

买了点数没有到账？

点数通常几秒内到账。没有的话，重新打开 Cue 会自动再试；还是没到，请发邮件给我们。

换了手机，或者重装了 Cue？

单词和对话存在你的 iCloud 里，会自动回来。点数账户存在 iCloud 钥匙串里，开着 iCloud 钥匙串，点数也会跟着回来。

隐私政策

Write down the English words, phrases or sentences you want to remember, or ask in Chinese how to say something. Cue writes an entry with AI, draws a picture, reads it aloud, and brings it back for review until it sticks.

Questions

How do points work?

A new word takes about 1 point (its entry and picture), a question 0.3 to 0.5, and a word someone else has added 0.2. Points don’t expire. You can also use your own OpenAI key in Settings, and OpenAI bills you directly.

Bought points that haven’t arrived?

Points usually arrive within seconds. If they don’t, open Cue again and it tries once more; if they still haven’t, email us.

New phone, or reinstalled Cue?

Your words and conversations are in your iCloud and come back by themselves. Your points account is in iCloud Keychain, so with iCloud Keychain on, your points come back too.
